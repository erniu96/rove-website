FAQ

Does Rove read my files?

No. Rove records only the names, sizes and dates of files and folders to build its search index, and the index stays on your Mac.

Why does Rove ask for Full Disk Access?

By default macOS keeps apps out of folders such as Mail and Messages. With Full Disk Access, Rove can include them in its index; without it Rove still works and simply skips those places.

How do I add my NAS?

Install Rove Agent on the NAS (Docker on Synology, QNAP, Unraid and others, or a one-line script on other models) and pair it in Rove › Settings › NAS. The list is made on the NAS and your Mac only downloads it; to open a file, connect to its share in Finder.

What does Rove cost? Can I try it first?

Yes. The 14-day free trial starts when you first open Rove, with every feature and no sign-up. After that, Rove is a one-time purchase of US$14.99 for up to 3 of your own Macs, including all 1.x updates. The index you built during the trial keeps working.

What happens when the trial ends, or I get a new Mac?

When the trial ends, searching stops but the index you built stays on your Mac; it is searchable again as soon as you activate a license key. For a new Mac, deactivate the old one in Settings › License or on the license page, then activate the new one.
